Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bingo Break free to use?

Yes, completely. No accounts, no subscriptions, no ads. Settings and scores are saved locally in your browser.

Can I customize the activities?

Absolutely. Flip either the Work or Break card to find the Edit button and replace any default with whatever fits your day.

What's the difference between 25/5 and 50/10?

Classic Pomodoro is 25 minutes of work, 5-minute break. The 50/10 variant suits people who need more runway to reach deep focus.

How does scoring work?

Work tasks earn 5× more than break activities. Both grids reward repeat completions with multipliers, and completing a full line or the whole board earns a bonus.

Will my progress reset if I close the browser?

No. Everything is saved to local storage as you go. Daily scores reset at midnight.

How do I add my own music?

Place MP3s in music/lofi/, music/guitar/, etc. with a manifest.json in each folder. The Music card lets you switch genres on the fly.
